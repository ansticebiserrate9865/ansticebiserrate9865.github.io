✨ What Is This Tool?

Have you ever found an amazing notebook on Kaggle and wished you could save it to your computer for offline reading or modification? pull-kaggle-notebooks does exactly that! It's a powerful yet simple tool that lets you download public notebooks from Kaggle datasets and automatically organizes them into neat folders.

Whether you're a student learning data science, a researcher gathering examples, or a developer wanting to study popular notebook patterns, this tool saves you hours of manual copy-pasting work.
